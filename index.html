Question 1

Initial Strategy & Approach

How we plan to engage this employer from day one

01

Target the Right Veteran Profile

Focus on senior NCOs (E-6 to E-7) with industrial or operations backgrounds. This employer needs proven supervisors — not entry-level candidates. Pre-screen through internal DVOP/LVER networks.

02

Host a Targeted Hiring Event

Use the job center to organize a veteran-specific hiring event. Post on Facebook, create a flyer, and leverage MO Jobs portal. Coordinate logistics including an HR rep on-site.

03

Educate the Employer on Military Rank & KSAs

Owner is new to hiring veterans. Walk them through the rank structure, translate military experience into civilian terms, and reinforce the built-in KSAs veterans bring — leadership, discipline, adaptability.

04

Do Market Research First

It's a new company. Review labor market data, investor backing, and industry projections before pitching veterans on the role. We need to be able to say "this is a solid opportunity" with confidence.

Question 2

Concerns & How We'll Address Them

Red flags and how we're thinking about handling them

⚠ Company Stability — It's brand new

How do we tell a veteran this is a solid long-term opportunity if the company hasn't proven itself yet?

FIX

Lead with the 2 years of secured orders. Pull LMI data to show the sector's growth trajectory. Frame it as a ground-floor opportunity with supervisor-level upside.

⚠ Employer Doesn't Understand Military Experience

Owner may not recognize the value of rank, MOS, or military KSAs on a resume — or may undervalue them.

FIX

Proactively educate before the first candidate is sent. Translate rank into civilian equivalents. Show how an E-7 maps to a floor supervisor or operations lead.

⚠ Resume Translation for the Veteran

Veterans sometimes write resumes in military language — acronyms, rank titles, mission language — that civilian employers can't parse.

FIX

Run resumes through a civilian translation process before submission. Use tools (ChatGPT, resume builders) to convert MOS experience into industry-relevant language.

⚠ Very High Skill Bar

Employer wants someone with strong industrial AND leadership skills — a narrow target population.

FIX

Be upfront about the difficulty of the match. Use all available networks — DVOP/LVER caseloads, job fairs, MO Jobs, Facebook — to cast a wide but targeted net.

Question 3

What We Still Need to Learn

Questions to ask the employer + research to do before our next conversation

🤝

Ask the Employer

  • What are your 5–10 year growth projections?
  • What does the full compensation package look like — base pay, medical, dental, retirement?
  • Are you able to accommodate VA medical appointment leave?
  • What does a typical week look like for this role — travel, shifts, overtime?
  • Have you worked with any staffing partners or workforce agencies before?
  • What does success look like in the first 90 days?
🔍

Research to Do

  • Pull LMI data for light manufacturing in this region — 3–5 year outlook
  • Review investor info / business filings if available — confirm company stability
  • Check the company's social media / website presence
  • Review any WOTC or hiring incentive eligibility
  • Identify veteran candidates in our caseload with industrial / supervisory background
  • Connect with DVOP/LVER counterparts in surrounding areas for pipeline support
